# 1099s

- Decide every vendor with books.set_vendor_1099 and who decided: nec, misc, or not_reportable with its reason_kind and the facts (a corporation, the United States or a state, a payee selling only merchandise...); undetermined is "not decided yet". "Paid by card" is never a vendor's reason. Never corporation for a payee paid for legal services or for medical and health care: those payments stay reportable even to a corporation (nec or misc). Give effective only when a payee's status changed (an LLC's corporate election, from its Form 8832 date); a payee that incorporates is a new vendor with its own W-9. Ask the human; the CPA confirms.
- Record each way the company pays once (books.add_instrument: the bank, the company card, each member's own card, each member's own cash or checks, a payment platform), and tag every payment to a vendor with its vendor or party (books.draft_entry vendor or party, books.set_entry_vendor, books.set_entry_party; books.pay_bill tags automatically).
- Tag what members paid for the company with the card or the cash they used (books.set_entry_instrument): their card is excluded, their own check counts. A card that only funded a friends-and-family transfer is their cash. Tag what a payment platform's balance paid the same way: goods and services excluded, friends-and-family counts; untagged, both stay undetermined.
- Card and payment-network payments are excluded (the processor's Form 1099-K), whoever's card it was. A bank payment with no method recorded counts: tag a debit-card payment with the card.
- books.vendor_1099 is the year-end worksheet for the CPA. undetermined means "tell me how it was paid" or "decide this vendor". With include_tins: true it carries the full TINs from the vault; csv: true also hands it over as a CSV file (run its download command to save it).
