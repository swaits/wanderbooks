# Payroll taxes
IRS Publication 15 (2026), Circular E. Governs the payroll entries (employer and employee FICA). wanderbooks records payroll; the withholding numbers come from the caller.

**Rates**

> For 2026, the social security tax rate is 6.2% (amount withheld) each for the employer and employee (12.4% total). The social security wage base limit is $184,500. The tax rate for Medicare is 1.45% (amount withheld) each for the employee and employer (2.9% total). There is no wage base limit for Medicare tax; all covered wages are subject to Medicare tax.

**Deposits**

> Generally, you must deposit federal income tax withheld and both the employer and employee social security and Medicare taxes.
