## 0. The one change that dissolves most of the design blockers: drop "reversal as a draft"
**Blocker. P4 971-993; I-4/I-5 (64-65); 4.9 513; 5.2 574; DC-A2, DC-A3, DC-B3, ST-F4, comprehension F1/F3/F5/E5.**
No owner decision asks for a reversal to be a draft: top decision 5 and D-12 say old reversals borrow the original's receipt; the live handbook and `ReceiptPolicy` say reversals need none; S1 built `void_document` as an immediate reversal and the owner upgraded to it. A reversal stays previewed-then-posted in one call, every reversal check at the moment it posts (what `check_reversal` already does), evidence = the original's by rule; evidence a reversal genuinely needs of its own (a returned-item notice for a bounce) is attached right after, as every at-once tool works today. Delete from P4: `entry.reversal_drafted`, `Step.draftReversal`, `evidence: link_original|{ids}|later`, the "reversal draft needs evidence" attention item, `reversal_needs_evidence`. Rewrite I-4: "Corrections are posted reversals; a reversal's evidence is its original's." `inherit_evidence` becomes an audit annotation only (D-12 names it: keep as a one-line event for the re-audit).

## 1. The critic's mechanisms, one by one
- **1a `settlement.posted` (DC-A4): over-engineered.** Replay already tags "the first untagged control line for `amount`" (ledger.rs:6466). Simpler: add `line` (and `discount_line`) indices to the existing `invoice.payment_received`/`bill.paid` events under `settlements`; the tool writes lines in a fixed order (payment lines in application order, then discount lines).
- **1b `Movement::Recovery` + role (DC-A11): YAGNI.** Handbook rule: same tax year, reverse the write-off then `receive_payment`; prior year, a plain entry Dr deposit / Cr "Bad debt recoveries" (revenue, tax category `other_income`), memo naming the invoice; the document stays Written off. Record as a decision.
- **1c `receive_payment.excess_to` (DC-A6): contradicts answer 5 and is unnecessary.** Replacement-before-bounce works today: park the cash Dr 1000 / Cr 2300 (no role, so allowed); reverse payment 1 dated the bounce day; `receive_payment {deposit_to: 2300, date ≥ bounce}` (`check_payment_account` accepts a role-less liability; bounds pass). Write it as a handbook procedure.
- **1d discounts on asset-line bills (DC-A7): take the second option.** Refuse a discount on a bill with an asset or prepaid line, remedy in the error (record the bill net, or reclassify Dr 6995 / Cr 1500). Record the decision.
- **1e the fee (DC-A5): Blocker on the document, Major on the scope.** The fee goes in S6. One kernel identity check with Kani (Σcash + Σdiscount == Σapplication; deposit == Σcash − fee); do not refactor `payout_plan`. Copy the payout pattern for the fee line (same expense account payouts use). `receive_payment {…, fee?: {amount, account?}}`, one posting row, behind `settlements`.
- **1f `adopt_control_line` variants (DC-A8): defer the whole tool.** P4b.14 item 4 already gives zero-code remedies (reverse and re-enter; or leave it with a decision and a CPA README note, which the identity's `unattached` term exists for). Build it only if `books.control_check` on the live books lists a line neither remedy can take. Same for `assign_role move_balance` (B-23 already says a control role with a balance stays). S7 shrinks to `write_off_invoice` + `bad_debts` role + B-41.
- **1g `owed_on` per-account identity (DC-A9): falls away with 1f.**
- **1h evidence gate (DC-A10/B2): right direction, four times too big.** Three moves: (1) move the policy evaluation from `ledger-config` into `ledger-core` as one function over `(&Ledger, &JournalEntry, &Settings)`; exemptions by origin use what `Ledger` already knows (closing entries, schedule postings, depreciation runs); (2) `Store::post_entry` calls it; (3) the GUI calls the same function over its Snapshot and deletes the string match at books.rs:708. At-once tools stay "post at once, attach evidence right after" with the existing `missing_receipt` item; the invoice carries its PDF and the bill its upload anyway (S4).
- **1i two-shape HMAC `preview_token` (DC-B1): one digest, no key, one choke point.** Every preview result (a draft's `preview_entry`, and every tool called without `confirmed`) returns `preview: sha256(canonical preview payload)` (draft: canonical draft state; at-once operation: canonical args + journal head seq); every confirm takes `preview`, recomputes, refuses `stale_preview` on mismatch. Computed in the result envelope, not per tool; no key file; required from the P4 sweep on (no "one release" grace).
- **1j jobs as lease holders (DC-C2): remove with `books.job`.** 3.9 as built lets a running request finish before a close.
- **1k audit split (DC-C3): accept the "current head" definition (a sentence), not a layered architecture.** Pure checks beside `Ledger::control_check`; `Store::verify`/`verify_blobs`/access-log chain in the store; the tool composes. One `audit.run {through, through_seq, workpaper_attachment, findings}` event.
